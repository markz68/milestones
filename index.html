Automatic sync runs while the app is open, visible, online, and connected. It waits while this panel is open. Sync now is always available when connected. Reconnect after a reload or Google session expiry.

How sync works

Checks run after saved edits, when you return to the app or regain internet access, and about once a minute while visible. Additions, edits, and deletions sync. Conflicting edits or an initial merge may need review with Sync now. Independently created events stay separate. Keep both devices’ clocks correct.

Milestones snapshots are separate from Infinity Bottle. Disconnecting this session keeps your local events and cloud snapshots.

Local backups

JSON includes your events, date precision, and deletion history. Keep a copy outside this browser. Import merges newer edits and saved deletions for matching IDs.

Advanced recovery

Export your current events first. These options change the local event list and can then sync to your other devices.

Check older local events merges any retained pre-sync copy, preserving newer edits and deletions. Restore previous local state restores the single undo point from the last change. It is replaced by the next change and is not an independent backup.
