PERSONAL TRACKING APPS BY MARK ZUMKELLER

Milestones & Infinity Bottle

Two independent, installable web apps hosted on GitHub Pages. Start with local storage and optionally connect your own Google Drive to move your records between devices.

Milestones

Remember important dates, count down to upcoming events, and track time since birthdays, anniversaries, home projects, trips, and other milestones. Supports approximate dates, yearly repeats, categories, icons, and JSON backups.

Google Drive sync can run automatically while Milestones is open, visible, online, and connected. Reconnect after reloading or when Google access expires.

Open Milestones

Infinity Bottle / Infinity Bourbon

Record whiskey additions and pours, track remaining bottle volume and composition, and estimate the blend's proof. Export JSON backups or CSV files.

Google Drive sync is manual. Sync before switching devices and before editing on another device.

Open Infinity Bottle

Your account, your records

Both apps use the same Google authorization project but keep separate data files. Each user's cloud records are stored in that user's own Google Drive application-data space. Neither app requests Gmail access or access to your ordinary Drive documents. Keep independent JSON backups.

Support and privacy questions: Milestones issues or Infinity Bottle issues. These are public pages. Do not post personal records, passwords, tokens, or other private information.
