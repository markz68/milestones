MILESTONES & INFINITY BOTTLE

Privacy policy

Updated October 21, 2026. This policy covers the Milestones and Infinity Bottle / Infinity Bourbon web apps maintained by Mark Zumkeller at markz68.github.io.

Information you enter

Milestones stores event names, dates, notes, sources, categories, icons, preferences, and edit/deletion history. Infinity Bottle stores additions, pours, whiskey names, amounts, proof, dates, notes, and sync metadata. The apps save working records in your browser's local storage. Exported backups are saved wherever you choose to keep them.

Optional Google Drive access

Connecting Google grants the drive.appdata permission to read and write app-specific data in your own Google Drive application-data folder. The apps use this access only for their backup and synchronization features. They do not request access to Gmail, contacts, or ordinary Drive documents.

Milestones also obtains your Drive account identifier and, when available, email address to identify the connected account and prevent accidental syncing to a different account. Its account binding and last-sync metadata are saved locally. Google access tokens stay in page memory; the apps do not save a Google password, client secret, or refresh token.

Milestones and Infinity Bottle share a Google authorization project and application-data permission, with separate filenames. They are not separate OAuth security boundaries. Revoking the shared permission may disconnect both apps.

How data is used and shared

The app code processes your records in your browser and sends them directly to Google when you use Drive sync. There is no developer-operated backend collecting your event or bottle records. The apps do not include advertising or analytics trackers and do not sell records, send them to advertisers, or use them to train AI models.

Google processes sign-in and Drive requests under its own policies. GitHub hosts the apps and may process standard request information, such as IP addresses, under its policies. Connecting Drive loads Google's authorization library. Visiting source repositories or public support links is a separate interaction with GitHub.

Use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

Retention, backups, and deletion

Local records remain until you remove them, replace them, or clear the browser's site storage. Disconnecting a session does not delete records. Clearing storage for markz68.github.io can affect other apps on that same origin, so export backups first.

Deleting a Milestones event creates a deletion record for synchronization. Older cloud snapshots can retain previous event content; deleting an event is not a purge of cloud history. Infinity Bottle keeps its current cloud history until it is replaced or its app data is removed. The apps do not automatically erase all cloud data when you disconnect.

To stop Google access, remove the app connection through your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revocation alone is not a promise of data deletion. Google Drive's settings offer management of hidden app data; deleting data for the shared Google project can affect both apps. Export both apps' backups before doing this. Downloaded JSON and CSV files must be removed separately from wherever you saved or shared them.

Security and your choices

Drive sync is optional; local use does not require connecting Google. HTTPS protects network transfers. Records are not encrypted with a separate app-specific key. Other code hosted on the same website origin shares the browser's storage security boundary. Protect your device and Google account, and keep independent backups.

Questions and policy changes

For questions, use Milestones issues or Infinity Bottle issues. These are public. Do not attach private records or credentials. Changes to data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.
